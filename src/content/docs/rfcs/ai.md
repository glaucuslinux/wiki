---
title: ai
description: An opinionated Linux® distribution based on musl libc and toybox
---

- The following packages are known to use or have probably used `ai` regardless of the use case:
  - `cmake`
  - `curl`
  - `expat`
  - `finit`? (`keventd` curates files from `eudev` which uses `ai`)
  - `glib`
  - `linux-headers`
  - `linux-lts`
  - `llvm`
  - `llvm-runtimes`
  - `nim`
  - `nim-csources`
  - `patch` for `neatvi` to support arrow keys
  - `python`
  - `tmux`
  - `u-config`
  - `util-linux`
  - `yash`
